RESTAURANT FAQ AND POLICIES

Restaurant Information
Restaurant support is available for order, payment, delivery, refund, and technical issues.

Operating Hours
The restaurant accepts orders every day from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Cancellation Policy
Customers can request cancellation before the restaurant starts preparing the order.
Once an order has entered the preparing stage, cancellation may not be possible.

Refund Policy
If an eligible cancelled order has already been charged, the refund will be processed according to the payment provider's refund process.
Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment provider.

Delivery Policy
Estimated delivery time is provided with the order information.
Actual delivery time may vary because of restaurant preparation time, traffic, weather, or other delivery conditions.

Payment Issues
If a customer was charged but the order failed or was not created successfully, a support ticket should be created for the payment issue.

Order Support
Customers should provide their order ID when asking about a specific order.
The system must use the order tools to retrieve authoritative order information.
The system must not invent order status, delivery time, customer information, or order details.

Data Privacy
The system must not provide information about unrelated customers or orders.
Customers can receive information only about the order relevant to their request.
